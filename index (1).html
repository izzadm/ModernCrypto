How to use this

  • Blue boxes are the definitions you may be asked to state verbatim. Learn the formulas, not the prose around them.
  • Red boxes are attacks. For each cipher, know the attack model it breaks under (ciphertext-only, known-plaintext, chosen-plaintext) and the cost.
  • The key-size and complexity numbers (26!, 256, 257, 112-bit) are the most commonly examined facts in this material. Learn them cold.
  • Every page prints cleanly — Ctrl/Cmd+P drops the navigation and colours the boxes for paper.
